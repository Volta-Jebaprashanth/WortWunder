# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

WortWunder is a learning project used by people of every age, background and
language level, including children. We, as contributors and maintainers,
pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps create a positive environment:

- Being welcoming and patient, especially with people who are new to German,
  to programming, or to open source
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for learners and the community

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Content that is inappropriate for a learning app used by children
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable
behaviour and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem harmful.

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour can be
reported privately to the project maintainer through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/Volta-Jebaprashanth). All reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including issues, pull
requests and discussions, and when an individual is officially representing
the project in public spaces.
